## Service Guides

### Tarot Readings

#### How to Get a Reading

1. **Navigate** to Tarot section
2. **Choose** your spread type:
   - **One Card**: Quick daily guidance
   - **Three Cards**: Past, Present, Future
   - **Five Cards**: Cross spread for deeper insight
   - **Celtic Cross**: Comprehensive 10-card reading
3. **Ask** your question (be specific!)
4. **Draw** your cards
5. **Receive** AI interpretation

#### Tips for Better Readings

- **Be Specific**: Instead of "Will I be happy?", ask "What should I focus on to improve my relationship?"
- **Stay Open**: Don't force a specific answer
- **Reflect**: Take time to understand the message
- **Journal**: Record readings for pattern recognition
- **Use Clarify**: Ask follow-up questions with AI chat (Premium/Platinum)

#### Understanding Card Positions

**Three-Card Spread:**
- Position 1: Past influences
- Position 2: Current situation
- Position 3: Future outcome

**Celtic Cross:**
- Position 1: Present situation
- Position 2: Challenge/obstacle
- Position 3: Foundation/root cause
- Position 4: Recent past
- Position 5: Possible future
- Position 6: Near future
- Position 7: Your approach
- Position 8: External influences
- Position 9: Hopes and fears
- Position 10: Final outcome

#### Reversed Cards

When enabled, reversed cards offer alternative or blocked energy:
- **Upright**: Direct, flowing energy
- **Reversed**: Blocked, internal, or opposite meaning

### Natal Chart

#### Getting Your Chart

1. **Enter** accurate birth information
2. **Select** house system (Placidus recommended)
3. **Calculate** your chart
4. **Explore** planets, houses, and aspects

#### Understanding Your Chart

**Planets** represent different life areas:
- ☉ **Sun**: Core identity, ego, vitality
- ☽ **Moon**: Emotions, instincts, subconscious
- ☿ **Mercury**: Communication, thinking, learning
- ♀ **Venus**: Love, beauty, values
- ♂ **Mars**: Action, desire, energy
- ♃ **Jupiter**: Growth, luck, expansion
- ♄ **Saturn**: Structure, discipline, lessons
- ♅ **Uranus**: Innovation, rebellion, change
- ♆ **Neptune**: Dreams, spirituality, illusion
- ♇ **Pluto**: Transformation, power, rebirth

**Houses** represent life areas:
- **1st**: Self, appearance, first impressions
- **2nd**: Money, possessions, values
- **3rd**: Communication, siblings, short trips
- **4th**: Home, family, roots
- **5th**: Creativity, romance, children
- **6th**: Health, work, daily routines
- **7th**: Partnerships, marriage, contracts
- **8th**: Transformation, shared resources, intimacy
- **9th**: Philosophy, travel, higher education
- **10th**: Career, public image, ambitions
- **11th**: Friends, groups, hopes
- **12th**: Spirituality, secrets, subconscious

**Aspects** show planetary relationships:
- **Conjunction** (0°): Blending energies
- **Sextile** (60°): Harmonious opportunity
- **Square** (90°): Tension, challenge
- **Trine** (120°): Easy flow, talent
- **Opposition** (180°): Balance, awareness

## Tips & Best Practices

### Getting the Most from Mirastros

#### 1. Accurate Birth Data
- Use exact birth time if possible
- Verify birth location coordinates
- Update if you discover more accurate info

#### 2. Ask Better Questions
**Instead of:** "Will I be rich?"
**Ask:** "What steps should I take to improve my financial situation?"

**Instead of:** "Does he love me?"
**Ask:** "What can I do to improve communication in my relationship?"

#### 3. Timing Matters
- **New Moon**: Set intentions, start projects
- **Full Moon**: Release, complete, celebrate
- **Mercury Retrograde**: Review, revise, avoid major contracts
- **Your Birthday Month**: Solar return energy, new beginnings

#### 4. Combine Services
- Check horoscope before tarot reading
- Review transits when interpreting dreams
- Use numerology with natal chart
- Consult lunar calendar for ritual timing

#### 5. Trust Your Intuition
- AI provides guidance, not commands
- You have free will
- Use readings as tools for reflection
- Trust your inner wisdom

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Reading Too Often**: Daily tarot on same question
✅ **Better**: Wait at least a week between readings on same topic

❌ **Ignoring Context**: Taking predictions literally
✅ **Better**: Consider your current life situation

❌ **Seeking Certainty**: Wanting definite yes/no
✅ **Better**: Seeking guidance and perspective

❌ **Passive Approach**: Waiting for fate
✅ **Better**: Using insights for active growth
