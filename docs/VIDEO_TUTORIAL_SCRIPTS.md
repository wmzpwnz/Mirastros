## Tutorial 2: Your First Tarot Reading
**Duration:** 7-10 minutes
**Target Audience:** Beginners

### Script

**[INTRO - 0:00-0:30]**

"Welcome back to Mirastros tutorials! Today, we're diving into one of our most popular features - Tarot readings. Whether you're completely new to tarot or have some experience, this tutorial will help you get meaningful, accurate readings from Mirastros."

**[SECTION 1: Understanding Tarot - 0:30-2:00]**

[Visual: Tarot deck overview]

"Tarot is a 78-card deck divided into:
- 22 Major Arcana cards - representing major life themes and spiritual lessons
- 56 Minor Arcana cards - representing day-to-day experiences

[Show card examples]

Each card has symbolic imagery that speaks to your subconscious. The AI interprets these symbols in the context of your question and your personal astrological profile."

**[SECTION 2: Choosing Your Spread - 2:00-3:30]**

[Visual: Spread types]

"Mirastros offers four spread types:

**One Card** - Quick daily guidance
Best for: Morning intentions, simple questions

**Three Card** - Past, Present, Future
Best for: Understanding a situation's timeline

**Five Card** - Cross Spread
Best for: Deeper insight into challenges

**Celtic Cross** - 10 cards
Best for: Complex situations, major decisions

For this tutorial, let's use the Three Card spread."

**[SECTION 3: Asking Better Questions - 3:30-5:00]**

[Visual: Question examples]

"The quality of your question affects the quality of your reading. Let's compare:

❌ Bad: 'Will I be happy?'
✅ Good: 'What can I do to cultivate more happiness in my life?'

❌ Bad: 'Does he love me?'
✅ Good: 'What's the energy between us and how can I improve our connection?'

❌ Bad: 'Should I quit my job?'
✅ Good: 'What should I consider regarding my career path?'

Notice the pattern? Good questions:
- Focus on what YOU can do
- Ask 'what' or 'how' instead of 'will'
- Seek guidance, not commands
- Are specific but open-ended"

**[SECTION 4: Performing the Reading - 5:00-7:30]**

[Visual: Live reading demonstration]

"Let's do a reading together. My question is: 'What should I focus on in my creative projects this month?'

[Select Three Card spread]
[Draw cards]

Here are my cards:
- Past: Seven of Cups - Many options, scattered energy
- Present: Three of Pentacles - Collaboration, skill-building
- Future: Ace of Wands - New creative spark, inspiration

[Read AI interpretation]

The AI tells me that my past scattered energy is now focusing through collaboration, leading to a burst of new creative inspiration. It suggests I work with others and stay focused on one project.

[Show how to save reading]

I can save this to my journal for future reference."

**[SECTION 5: Using Clarify Feature - 7:30-9:00]**

[Visual: AI chat demonstration]

"Premium and Platinum members can click 'Clarify with Mirastros' to dig deeper.

[Click button, show chat]

I'll ask: 'Which creative project should I prioritize?'

[Show AI response]

The AI considers my natal chart and the reading context to provide personalized guidance. This is where Mirastros really shines - combining traditional tarot wisdom with AI intelligence and your personal astrological profile."

**[OUTRO - 9:00-10:00]**

"Remember:
- Choose the right spread for your question
- Ask empowering, open-ended questions
- Reflect on the interpretation
- Use Clarify for deeper insights
- Journal your readings to track patterns

In our next tutorial, we'll explore your natal chart and learn how it influences all your readings.

Happy reading, and may the cards guide you well!"
