## Services

### Tarot

**Q: How often can I do tarot readings?**
A: 
- Freemium: 1 per week
- Premium/Platinum: Unlimited
- Best practice: Wait at least a week between readings on the same question

**Q: Can I choose specific cards?**
A: No, cards are drawn randomly to ensure authentic divination. The randomness is part of the tarot's wisdom.

**Q: What's the difference between spread types?**
A:
- **One Card**: Quick daily guidance
- **Three Card**: Past-Present-Future or Situation-Action-Outcome
- **Five Card**: Cross spread for deeper insight
- **Celtic Cross**: Comprehensive 10-card reading for complex situations

**Q: Should I allow reversed cards?**
A: It's personal preference. Reversed cards add nuance but can be more complex to interpret. Beginners might start without reversals.

**Q: Can I get a reading for someone else?**
A: Yes, but focus on your relationship with them or how you can help, not on controlling their choices.

### Astrology

**Q: What's the difference between sun sign and natal chart?**
A:
- **Sun Sign**: Just your zodiac sign (where the Sun was at birth)
- **Natal Chart**: Complete map of ALL planets, houses, and aspects at your exact birth time and location

Your natal chart is much more accurate and comprehensive.

**Q: Why is my horoscope different from other apps?**
A: Most apps provide generic sun sign horoscopes. Our Premium/Platinum personalized horoscopes are based on YOUR complete natal chart, making them more accurate and specific to you.

**Q: What's a transit?**
A: A transit is when a current planet makes an aspect to a planet in your natal chart. For example, "Jupiter transiting your Sun" means Jupiter in the sky is forming an aspect to where your Sun was at birth.

**Q: What's Mercury Retrograde and should I worry?**
A: Mercury Retrograde occurs 3-4 times per year when Mercury appears to move backward. It's associated with:
- Communication mishaps
- Technology glitches
- Travel delays
- Contract issues

Don't panic! Use it for:
- Reviewing and revising
- Reconnecting with old friends
- Reflecting on the past
- Backing up data

Avoid if possible:
- Signing major contracts
- Buying electronics
- Starting new projects

**Q: What's a Saturn Return?**
A: Your Saturn Return occurs around age 29-30 (and again at 58-59) when Saturn returns to its position at your birth. It's a major life restructuring period often involving:
- Career changes
- Relationship evaluations
- Taking on adult responsibilities
- Facing fears and limitations

It's challenging but ultimately leads to maturity and authenticity.

### Numerology

**Q: How is numerology calculated?**
A:
- **Life Path**: Sum of birth date digits reduced to single digit
- **Destiny**: Sum of all letters in full birth name
- **Soul Urge**: Sum of vowels in name
- **Personality**: Sum of consonants in name

Example: Birth date 06/15/1995
6 + 1 + 5 + 1 + 9 + 9 + 5 = 36 → 3 + 6 = 9 (Life Path 9)

**Q: Should I use my married name or birth name?**
A: Use your birth name for core numbers (Destiny, Soul Urge, Personality). Your married name can be calculated separately to see how it influences your current energy.

**Q: What are Master Numbers?**
A: 11, 22, and 33 are Master Numbers with special significance:
- **11**: Intuition, inspiration, spiritual messenger
- **22**: Master builder, manifesting dreams into reality
- **33**: Master teacher, compassionate service

Don't reduce these to single digits.

### Dreams

**Q: Why should I interpret my dreams?**
A: Dreams are your subconscious mind communicating through symbols. They can reveal:
- Hidden emotions
- Unresolved issues
- Creative solutions
- Spiritual messages
- Future possibilities

**Q: I don't remember my dreams. What can I do?**
A:
- Keep journal by bedside
- Set intention before sleep: "I will remember my dreams"
- Don't move immediately upon waking
- Write fragments, even if incomplete
- Avoid alcohol before bed
- Get enough sleep

**Q: Do dreams predict the future?**
A: Some dreams can be precognitive, but most are symbolic representations of your current psychological state. Use them for insight, not literal prediction.

**Q: What if I have recurring nightmares?**
A: Recurring nightmares often indicate:
- Unresolved trauma
- Persistent anxiety
- Ignored issues

If nightmares are severe or affecting your life, consider professional therapy alongside dream interpretation.

### Runes & Divination

**Q: What's the difference between Tarot, Runes, I Ching, and Lenormand?**
A:
- **Tarot**: 78 cards, rich symbolism, psychological depth
- **Runes**: 24 Norse symbols, direct, action-oriented
- **I Ching**: 64 hexagrams, philosophical, strategic
- **Lenormand**: 36 cards, practical, concrete answers

Choose based on your question type and personal resonance.

**Q: Can I use multiple divination methods for the same question?**
A: Yes, but:
- Don't "shop" for the answer you want
- Different methods offer different perspectives
- Wait at least a day between methods
- Look for common themes, not contradictions

### Crystals

**Q: Do I need to buy crystals from your shop?**
A: No! We provide recommendations, but you can source crystals anywhere. What matters is your intention and connection with the stone.

**Q: How do I cleanse and charge crystals?**
A:
- **Cleanse**: Moonlight, sage smoke, running water, salt
- **Charge**: Sunlight, moonlight, intention, earth burial
- **Program**: Hold and state your intention

**Q: Can crystals really heal?**
A: Crystals work on an energetic level. They're complementary tools, not medical treatments. Always seek professional medical care for health issues.

- Update to latest version

- Check internet connection

### I can't make a payment

### I was charged twice

This is usually a temporary authorization hold. If you see two charges:
- Wait 3-5 business days
- Check if one is "pending"
